# CIS implementation handoff — independent chart framework v1

Date: 23 October 2026. Test round: ISRG, FICO, CRDO and PODD, now complete as a functional/design exercise. This is a reviewable implementation specification, not a production deployment or proof of a profitable trading strategy.

## 1. Product objective and decisions to preserve

The user wants an independent chart judgment that answers three different questions:

1. Is price operating in constructive levels, testing support, or already deteriorating?
2. If price falls, where are the constructive floor, warning, tactical failure and major-damage thresholds?
3. If price rises, which actual ceilings must be cleared, and what evidence would show that resistance is becoming support?

Every side must include its own visible **Why this read**, backed by dated prices and volume. Do not substitute a BUY/SELL badge, a list of moving averages, analyst targets or a generic trailing-profit strategy. Upward analysis means a ceiling ladder. Final user-facing names remain undecided; use neutral working labels **If price falls** and **If price rises**. Keep stable internal identifiers `downside` and `upside` so later title changes are presentation-only.

The user approved the floor/ceiling method after seeing the first three names and requested PODD as the final example. PODD's specific proposed levels are newly presented with this packet; do not represent them as separately user-approved.

No production changes, notifications, portfolio edits or orders were made. This document requests implementation work; it does not authorize an automatic merge, deployment or trading execution.

## 2. The important implementation boundary

**The examples' levels were selected by analyst judgment from actual chart evidence. They were not generated by a validated universal level-selection algorithm.** Do not claim that a new heuristic exactly reproduces that judgment.

Implement v1 as deterministic measurement, an explicit versioned level packet, a deterministic state evaluator and the explanation UI. Accept approved/proposed analyst level packets with provenance. A future candidate generator may propose swings, clusters and average confluence, but its levels must be marked `code_candidate_unreviewed` until reviewed. Candidate generation and automatic approval are separate capabilities.

This makes the work concrete without hiding the current methodological gap. CIS can build and test the engine against the supplied packets now. Fully automatic selection for arbitrary new tickers needs a separate specification, parameter review and validation. Do not silently promote a nearest moving average or a support below current price to a healthy floor.

## 3. Data contract and time boundary

- Daily OHLCV, symbol/exchange/currency, adjustment convention, retrieval time, source endpoint and raw SHA-256. Retain source snapshots for reproducibility.
- Stock and SPY must share the same completed-session boundary. Add a relevant sector ETF, explicitly labeled a proxy. Missing sector evidence stays unavailable.
- Prefer about six years of raw history to initialize indicators and search the most recent five years; use only actual listed history. No inferred pre-IPO prices.
- Reject nonfinite/nonpositive OHLC, malformed ranges, negative volume, duplicate or descending daily dates and non-daily granularity. Do not fill missing observations. Zero-volume bars require explicit handling, not invented volume.
- A requested `interval=1d` is not sufficient: verify returned granularity. An initial Yahoo `range=max` request returned monthly data during this exercise and was discarded; explicit timestamp requests returned daily data.
- Match the price-adjustment convention throughout. In these fixtures OHLC = vendor OHLC × adjusted-close/close; vendor volume is retained. Current adjusted history is not a point-in-time archive. Query corporate actions and disclose the limits of that audit.
- A daily bar is complete only after the relevant exchange session has ended. Use exchange calendar and New York timestamps, including holidays and early closes. The examples conservatively exclude the current date's daily row.
- Separate `last_completed_session`, `quote_timestamp`, `current_session_low/high` and retrieval timestamp. Never substitute a live quote into completed indicators or historical outcomes.
- If a calendar is unavailable, say so. Do not invent a calendar endpoint or treat every weekday as an exchange session. Weekly bars may be labeled by week-ending Friday even when the last actual session was Thursday; keep both dates. In this test 3 July 2026 was not a daily session; the relevant actual session was 2 July.
- Mark stale/misaligned feeds as data limitations; do not translate missing/stale data into a bearish score. Partial-session volume cannot be compared with a full-day average as if comparable.

## 4. Measurements to reproduce

Use versioned indicator settings and preserve adequate initialization history:

- SMA20/50/200 = arithmetic rolling close averages.
- RSI14: exponential Wilder gain/loss smoothing, alpha 1/14, `adjust=False`; state the initialization. Zero loss with positive gain gives 100, zero gain with positive loss gives 0, a genuinely flat gain/loss history gives 50, and inadequate inputs give unavailable. These edge conventions must be explicit.
- ATR14: true range = max(high−low, abs(high−previous close), abs(low−previous close)); exponential alpha 1/14, `adjust=False`, initialized from available true ranges as in the reference calculations. Do not silently substitute an SMA-seeded variant.
- MACD = EMA12−EMA26, with EMA9 signal, `adjust=False`; expose line, signal, histogram and histogram change.
- Relative volume = signal-day volume / mean of the **previous 20 completed sessions**, excluding the signal day. Display both numerator and denominator. The old scanner includes the signal bar in its volume baseline; this independent calculation deliberately differs.
- Relative return over N aligned sessions = `(stock_end/stock_start)/(benchmark_end/benchmark_start)−1`, N=21/63, using common actual dates. Distinguish it from RSI.
- Price distance to averages and levels, ATR percentage, daily/weekly return, actual intraday high/low and close-only trailing highs with their definitions. Do not call a close-only maximum an intraday high.
- Completed weekly OHLCV, 10/40-week averages and an explicit incomplete-week flag.

Price/volume governs the read. Trend, location, participation, momentum, volatility and relative strength remain visible. Framework weights 30/20/20/15/10/5 organize judgment; displaying a combined score is optional. Do not force a score when evidence is absent, map that score to odds, or equate a high score with good entry location.

## 5. Versioned levels, not tick-driven moving thresholds

Each level packet needs an ID/version, symbol, as-of completed session, anchor/source IDs, selection method, review status, rounding policy and rationale. Each anchor records actual session date, OHLC/indicator field, value and the date it became available.

An active tactical plan points to a specific level-packet version. Updating a quote evaluates those levels; it must not recalculate them from every tick. An explicit new analysis version may propose new levels with a change log. Do not loosen an active position's protective failure level simply because price fell. Raising protection also requires an explicit structure-based revision; do not silently infer a new stop from price being higher.

Adjacent-level rounding must be coherent. Round a broad resistance band's outer edge outward if necessary to contain the anchor, and retain the unrounded anchor. For example, CRDO's high was 286.24, so the inspected outer band became 270–287 rather than treating a close at 286.10 as clearance of that high. A selected price zone is an analyst convention, not a one-cent physical barrier.

The old StockWatch plan had a reproduced branch discontinuity: with otherwise fixed captured data, 454.65→454.67 changed the mechanical stop from about 425.48 to 441.69 and changed targets/replay rates. **The new level evaluator must not repeat that behavior:** a tiny quote change can change the state only if it crosses an explicit fixed threshold; it cannot rewrite the level packet or replay barriers.

## 6. Downside state machine

Inputs: constructive interval `[floor_low, floor_high]`, warning, failure and major-damage thresholds. The fixtures use warning = floor_low. Require `major_damage < failure < warning = floor_low < floor_high`.

Evaluate the most severe strict-below condition first:

| Condition on completed close | State | Explanation-only response |
|---|---|---|
| close < major damage | `major_damage` | Broader base compromised; tactical failure already occurred |
| close < failure | `failure` | Tactical thesis invalidated; its exit rule applies |
| close < warning | `warning` | Constructive floor lost; stop adding, reduction review |
| floor_low ≤ close < floor_high | `testing_floor` | Need buyer defense; no automatic buy |
| close ≥ floor_high | `above_floor` | Above this setup's floor; entry quality and larger trend remain separate |

Use strict `<` for the phrase “below.” At an exactly equal threshold, do not report a below-threshold breach. A packet without a defensible constructive floor returns `undetermined`; never choose a lower support just to label the stock healthy.

Compute three distinct outputs:

1. `confirmed_state`: last completed close against the fixed packet.
2. `quote_state`: current valid quote against the same packet, labeled provisional.
3. `session_extreme_breaches`: which thresholds today's observed low crossed, and whether the quote has since recovered. A wick below a level followed by a quote above it is not a current below-level quote or a confirmed closing breach.

CRDO is the acceptance example: completed close 162.77 confirms warning; captured quote 153.01 indicates provisional failure; session low 142.89 crossed the 149 damage threshold but recovered above it. Do not show confirmed major damage.

A gap can skip several states. Evaluate the actual most severe state directly; do not require three separate daily observations before recognizing failure. A close-based plan is not a broker stop order and does not promise an execution price.

## 7. Upside ceiling state machine

Store ordered resistance intervals with distinct significance: first repair, constructive reclaim, average/price confluence, prior rally high and distant historical supply. Those descriptions are explanatory roles, not final setup titles or return probabilities.

- `below`: price remains below the zone.
- `testing`: price touches/enters it; an intraday wick is not a confirming close.
- `close_above`: a completed daily close exceeds its upper edge. Follow-through and retest remain pending.
- `retest_defended`: a later completed session trades into the former resistance zone and closes back above its upper edge. Retain the dated evidence and assess continued defense.
- `failed_breakout`: after a confirming close above, a later completed close falls below the zone's lower edge. A close inside the band is unresolved/testing, not automatically a full failure.

The current quote or current-session wick can flag an attempt but cannot produce a completed `close_above`/`retest_defended`. Never claim a retest before one occurs. A gap above multiple ceilings can initially clear several at once; do not invent a visit or retest to each rung.

Successful resistance-to-support conversion creates a **candidate** new constructive floor. It does not automatically overwrite the active protective plan. Volume and context qualify breakout quality: stronger completed participation, follow-through, improving relative strength and a defended retest strengthen the case. A fixed 1.5× volume filter is an unvalidated policy choice if added; it is not an empirically proven universal cutoff.

Do not show the likelihood of reaching each ceiling without a separately specified and validated model. A prior price of 250 is evidence of a historical zone, not evidence that 250 will recur. Distances to zones are percentages from a timestamped anchor, not forecast gains.

## 8. Narrative and UI requirements

First view must show timestamped quote/completed close, daily/weekly context, current state/bias/confidence, a plain takeaway, both floor and ceiling ladders, entry-quality caveat and verified event badge. Do not equate “above floor” with “buy now.” A daily recovery can exist inside a bearish longer-term structure.

Use six working tabs: Overview, Key levels, Analysis & numbers, Scenarios, Past reactions, What changes. The Overview includes separate Why this read explanations for downside and upside. Every material observation connects actual dated evidence → interpretation → consequence, plus counterevidence. Formal patterns must have anchors/status/confirmation/invalidation; otherwise describe the structure without forcing a textbook name.

Actual adjusted OHLC candlesticks plus aligned volume, weekly context, meaningful annotations, hover/tap details, selectable levels and historical paths. Keep the data window and forecast horizon distinct. No fake candles, placeholder paths or timeframe controls without data. Support phone widths, accessible controls and text labels alongside colors.

Narratives may be templated from structured evidence. Do not infer unverified news causes, institutional intent or future price action. If an optional language model writes prose, constrain it to the supplied packet and require anchor IDs for every numeric claim. No model/API integration was implemented or selected in this test.

## 9. Probabilities and same-stock history

Three concepts stay separate: condition assessment, confidence in that assessment, and future scenario weights. No score-to-probability conversion.

Default horizon: 20 trading sessions. Anchor P0 = completed close. Default neutral band B = completed ATR14. Up endpoint > P0+B; sideways P0−B ≤ endpoint ≤ P0+B; down endpoint < P0−B. Fix the thresholds for the snapshot. Show a verified endpoint date only with an exchange calendar. A wider neutral band produces more sideways outcomes; endpoint sideways does not mean a quiet path.

In this test scenario weights were **subjective, not backtested or calibrated**, in 5-point increments summing to 100%. Store the method, weights, reasons/counterevidence and probability confidence. Do not generate weights automatically from technical scores or the four examples. Allow probability fields to be unavailable if evidence is inadequate. PODD's 35/35/30 is an analyst input for its snapshot, not a rule for all recoveries.

Historical analogues use only same-stock causal features, predeclared thresholds, fixed selection before outcomes and no optimization by returns. Retain the first qualifying episode then suppress 20 trading sessions for 20-session outcomes. Record any overlap in indicator contexts. Do not pool different methods or formation lengths.

For each match: signal date/close, feature similarity and differences, 5/10/20-session close returns, interim high/low excursion, fixed support test, reclaim definition/time and event context. Unobserved horizons are null, never zero. Full-sample counts, medians/ranges and endpoint categories must be visible. Match a historical neutral band using that date's own ATR if comparing frequencies. Rates are descriptions, not future probabilities.

The different ticker rules in `*-independent-evidence.json` were individually declared for each observed setup; no validated automatic rule-selector exists. Preserve those exact rules in fixtures. A production rule library and automatic selection would be new methodology. The isolated historical shape lab is a different method and must remain separately labeled; it did not establish an out-of-sample advantage in the ISRG audit.

For exact example ranking, normalized feature distances were additive absolute differences: ISRG RSI/15 plus distance-to-SMA200/0.06; FICO RSI/15 + closing-high drawdown/0.30 + rebound-from-20-session-low/0.40 + ATR-percent/0.05; CRDO RSI/15 + drawdown/0.30 + five-session-return/0.20 + ATR-percent/0.08; PODD RSI/15 + distance-to-SMA50/0.10 + drawdown/0.30 + ATR-percent/0.04. Divide the absolute historical-minus-query feature difference by each stated denominator. Selection takes the first qualifying day and suppresses the next 20 before ranking; it does not select winners by outcome. ISRG reserved the current 100-session context from historical outcome overlap; the other three reserved the current 20-session formation. Those are example-specific research choices, not one validated universal matcher. The bundle includes `ISRG-independent-evidence.json` alongside the separate scanner-reproduction audit; do not confuse the two historical methods.

## 13. Required acceptance checks

1. All four reference packets reproduce their state from the supplied fixed levels and completed close; do not claim the levels themselves were generated by code.
2. PODD is above its floor while its first ceiling remains uncleared; entry quality remains “stretched/limited room,” not automatic BUY.
3. CRDO: warning confirmed, failure provisional, major-damage wick recovered. Three facts cannot be collapsed into one confirmed damage flag.
4. Exact threshold equality does not activate a strict-below rule; gap moves can skip stages; changing a quote cannot mutate a packet.
5. The ISRG 454.65→454.67 regression leaves fixed independent levels unchanged. Changing plan/packet versions is explicit.
6. A ceiling wick cannot confirm a break; a confirming close cannot fabricate a retest; a later close inside a zone stays unresolved, while a close below its lower edge can invalidate the breakout.
7. Prior swing anchors requiring later bars become available only after those bars close; no future-established swing in a historical signal. Changing future rows cannot alter a past selection.
8. Indicator periods/initialization and prior-20 volume convention reproduce fixture values within documented tolerance. Missing/partial input remains unknown; invalid granularity is refused.
9. All historical endpoint/excursion arithmetic, per-horizon counts and full sample summaries reconcile. No cherry-picked examples or fabricated zero returns.
10. Scenario weights sum to 100, endpoint categories are exhaustive/exclusive, weights are not mapped from score, and the method/confidence/band remain visible.
11. Event source and date/session display correctly; no claim a probability model has incorporated earnings when it has not.
12. Tabs, level selection, historical selection and candle details work at 390px and desktop. A changing label cannot change the underlying numbers. No fake live refresh.
