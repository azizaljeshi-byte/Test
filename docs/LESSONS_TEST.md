# Lessons under test (31 Oct): intraday and swing pattern reading (not part of the live playbook until the owner approves)

These come from the owner's two guides. The charts in them are fictional examples, not evidence of an edge. Use them to read structure, not as buy signals.

## The central rule
Judge only the evidence visible at the review time. Never use later bars to justify an entry. Setup quality is not the outcome: a good setup can lose and a poor one can win, so grade the confirmation, the risk and the missing evidence.
Label every read at the time as: forming, confirmed, failed, or no clean pattern. Missing data stays unknown.

## Intraday clock (Quick)
Use the daily picture for overhead support and resistance, the 5-minute candles for the setup. A 1-minute bounce never overrides failed 5-minute structure. Use only completed candles.
Volume must match the clock: compare with the same time of day on earlier sessions (the data gives "vol_vs_slot"), never with a whole day.
Three families:
- Opening-range breakout: freeze the 09:30-09:45 high and low (given). Nothing before 09:45. A wick above the range is only an attempt. Require a completed 5-minute close above the range high AND an observed hold or retest that holds the level, with same-time volume and room before resistance. A completed close back inside the range after entry is adverse evidence. A wide opening range leaves little room.
- VWAP reclaim: regular-session VWAP from 09:30 (given as "vwap"). Require a completed reclaim above VWAP and then a support response on a retest. A flat VWAP with repeated crossings is chop, not trend. The reclaim can fail at once.
- Trend pullback: higher highs and higher lows on completed candles, a pullback with easing volume, buyers defending the pullback low, price above a rising VWAP. Do not chase the expansion. A deep pullback through structure that fails to recover weakens the case; repeated VWAP crosses mean a choppy market.
Say WAIT / NO TRADE when: VWAP is flat with repeated crossings, candles are unconfirmed, data is stale, volume is thin, little room before resistance, or an event is near.

## Swing patterns (New call, daily context)
- Breakout and retest: a completed close above resistance becomes evidence only when a retest is observed to hold.
- Uptrend pullback: orderly pause above prior swing support with easing selling; a moving-average touch alone is not enough.
- Base and reversal: record first low, rebound high, higher low; a completed reclaim of the intervening high is stronger than a bounce or a single big green candle. Failed reversals belong to the same family.
- Compression near resistance: a coil is not an entry. The break is still pending at review.
- Failed breakdown and reclaim: the reclaim must hold; a brief reclaim followed by another support loss is a failed recovery.
- Post-earnings continuation: separate the first reaction from a later entry; a gap fade can invalidate the thesis and gaps can go through stops.
